The Match Report

1st

Simon Edwards — Pending Moderation

Simon Edwards takes it. Pending Moderation, top of the pile, and it is emphatic. 57 points, the first name etched onto the trophy — provisionally, of course. His captain, Haaland, offered only a modest 4 points, but it didn't matter — this was a week won in the engine room, with Trafford quietly stacking 9 points between the sticks and a bench costing him a mere 7 points of regret. Composed. Controlled. A statement of intent.

2nd

Michael Ball — Sao Baulo

Michael Ball responds. Sao Baulo, into second, but this was no straightforward passage. Two auto-substitutions were required just to survive the week — Caicedo withdrawn, Sarr withdrawn, reinforcements rushed on. And yet 52 points stand on the board regardless. There is quality here, but there is also fragility, and fragility has a habit of being punished later in a season.

3rd

Daniel Dring — The Two Time

Daniel Dring completes the podium in third — but this is where the drama truly lives. The Two Time posted 51 points on the pitch, yet left a staggering 15 points marooned on the bench. Xhaka delivered 9 of those points from the sidelines, doing everything asked of him except actually being selected. A performance undermined by its own team sheet.

4th

Michael Desmond — Tony Yeboah's Eye

The nonconformist of the gameweek. While the chasing pack reached for Haaland, Desmond turned to Bruno Fernandes. The gamble returned an identical 4 points — no reward for individuality here — and 13 points sat unused on his bench courtesy of Kayode. Brave. Ultimately unrewarded.

5th

Joel Xavier — Jianguo FC

Held at 39 points, and the frustration will linger. Gyökeres was withdrawn via auto-substitution before he could make an impact, Bruno Fernandes offered the standard 4-point return, and an overall rank beyond 7 million tells its own story. A gameweek to forget, and quickly.

6th

Jonathan Kane — Real Moat

Rock bottom. The captain's armband went, predictably, to Haaland — 4 points, the going rate across the league — but it was the choice of Foden as vice-captain that will sting hardest, contributing a solitary point when it mattered. Nine points left stranded on the bench besides. The wooden spoon, in Week One, goes to Real Moat.

The Headlines

  • Pending Moderation sits top, and there is nothing provisional about that scoreline.
  • Four of six managers turned to Haaland for the armband. Four of six received precisely 4 points for their trust.
  • 15 points, gone, wasted, unclaimed — Daniel Dring's bench outscored some captains.
  • Real Moat, real questions to answer. The wooden spoon lands in Week One.
  • Not a single hit taken across the entire league. Nerve, or simply indecision.

One gameweek down. The gap is narrow, the questions are many.
