The Match Report

1st

Simon Edwards — Pending Moderation

The streak is over — and the timing couldn't be more on brand. Four gameweeks without a single transfer, and it ends with three at once: Porro out for Ajayi, Thiaw out for De Cuyper, Hughes out for Bogle. 52 points, the week's best, though 18 were left to rot on the bench — Rushworth alone banking 11 from a benched goalkeeper. Doesn't matter. Stays top of the league, extends the total to 325.

2nd

Joel Xavier — Jianguo FC

The wildcard hangover fades a little further. One clean transfer (Donnarumma out, Raya in), Haaland's standard return, and a tidy 7 points on the bench. 46 points continues the recovery from last week's low. Climbs to 3rd overall, +1.

3rd

Daniel Dring — The Two Time

Another moderate week from a manager still finding his post-bench-curse footing. 43 points, no transfers, 11 left unused with Barnes's 9 the main culprit — a step back from last week's tidy effort, but nowhere near the historic highs of 19, 15, and 14 earlier in the season. Holds at 5th overall.

4th

Jonathan Kane — Real Moat

A new low for bench management — not just for Real Moat, for the entire league this season. 31 points left unused, obliterating the previous single-week record of 19, with Brobbey's 17 alone doing most of the damage from the sidelines. A second hit taken too — another four points gone — for a net 40. Real Moat sits 6th yet again. The table still hasn't noticed, and this week it had good reason not to.

5th

Michael Ball — Sao Baulo

The first properly quiet week of the season. 38 points, well below the tidy, table-climbing form of the last three gameweeks, though a single transfer and just 7 on the bench mean the squad management itself wasn't the problem — the returns simply weren't there. Holds at 2nd overall regardless.

6th

Michael Desmond — Tony Yeboah's Eye

The week's worst score, and the week's worst captain to match. Palmer wore the armband again and returned just 2, doubled to 4 — a repeat of the pattern that's dogged this squad since the Bruno Fernandes days. 28 points the final tally, the lowest of anyone all season. Slips to 4th overall, -1.
