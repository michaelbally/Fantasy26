The Match Report

1st

Michael Ball — Sao Baulo

The quiet week is forgotten — Sao Baulo is back, and the leader should be looking over his shoulder. One transfer, and it was a beauty: Cash out for nothing, De Cuyper in for 15. Add Haaland's 12 as captain and 69 points is the week's best score. A bench of 13 (Evanilson 7, Ajer 6) is the only blemish. The gap to the top has shrunk from 26 points to 12 in a single gameweek. Holds 2nd, and dangerous.

2nd

Joel Xavier — Jianguo FC

A strong week with a ten-point hole in it. Isak out, João Pedro in, and the swap returned 12. But the armband went to Bruno Fernandes, who managed a single point, doubled to 2 — while Haaland, sitting there as vice-captain, delivered 6 that would have doubled to 12. That's ten points left on the table. 59 still beats the global average. Holds 3rd overall.

3rd

Michael Desmond — Tony Yeboah's Eye

From the season's lowest score to exactly double it. Last week's 28 becomes this week's 56, thanks largely to Rogers wearing the armband and returning 9, doubled to 18. A like-for-like swap of Mbeumo for Groß cost a couple of points, and 14 more sat on the bench — Kadıoğlu alone left 9 unclaimed. Holds 4th overall.

4th

Simon Edwards — Pending Moderation

Back to doing nothing, and still on top. After the brief flurry of three transfers last week, the squad stays untouched again. 55 points — below the global average of 58 — with Haaland's 12 as captain, a tidy 4 on the bench, and one auto-sub. Total of 380 keeps him first, but the lead over Sao Baulo is now just 12. The crown is wobbling.

5th

Jonathan Kane — Real Moat

The wildcard is finally played — all four swaps of it. Lewis-Potter, Tzolis, Foden and — at last — Brobbey are out, with Schade, Saka, Yarmoliuk and Kostoulas in. The most notable arrival, Yarmoliuk, scored 8 points straight onto the bench, where Verbruggen added 9 more: 19 points unused. 46 is a modest return for a chip, and Real Moat stays 6th, now only 4 points behind The Two Time.

6th

Daniel Dring — The Two Time

The week's lowest score, and the gap behind is closing fast. 42 points, no transfers — in fact, none since Gameweek 2 — with 9 left on the bench, Evanilson's 7 the main culprit. Haaland delivered the usual 12, but little else did. Holds 5th overall, but with Real Moat just 4 points behind, that position is far from safe.
