The Match Report

1st

Joel Xavier — Jianguo FC

The wildcard is played, and Jianguo FC tears up the form book entirely. Seventeen transfers in a single sitting, an entire squad rebuilt in an afternoon — and it worked. Haaland captained for 18, Isak brought in for 13, Ødegaard for 10. 70 points, the best of the week, though 11 more sat unclaimed on the bench courtesy of Egan. A serious statement — and a climb to 2nd overall.

2nd

Michael Ball — Sao Baulo

Matched the week's top score without any of the theatre. One quiet swap — Ndiaye out, Cherki in — and an auto-substitution that barely registered. 70 points, the same tally as the wildcard rebuild next door, but earned with a fraction of the fuss. Climbs to 4th overall. Understated, effective, dangerous.

3rd

Jonathan Kane — Real Moat

The best gameweek score of anyone still sat at the bottom of the table. 62 points, Haaland doing the heavy lifting again, two sensible transfers in — and yet Real Moat remains rooted to 6th overall, still nursing the lowest total in the league. Three gameweeks, three respectable scores — 39, then 69, now 62 — and the table still hasn't noticed.

4th

Daniel Dring — The Two Time

The bench misery continues, and it's now a pattern, not a blip. 56 points on the pitch, but 14 more left to rot on the sidelines — Barnes alone racked up 12 of them without being picked. Fifteen benched in Gameweek 1, nineteen in Gameweek 2, fourteen this week: The Two Time has now put together the league's worst bench management three gameweeks running. Slips to 5th overall. The team sheet remains the opponent.

5th

Michael Desmond — Tony Yeboah's Eye

Worst captain of the gameweek, and it isn't close — and what a fall from grace. Just one week ago, this same Bruno Fernandes armband returned 46 points and looked like managerial genius. This week: a solitary 2, doubled to 4, while Haaland quietly delivered 18 elsewhere across the league. 48 points the final tally. Slips to 3rd overall — still clinging on, for now, on the strength of that one golden week.

6th

Simon Edwards — Pending Moderation

Bottom of the gameweek pile — and still, somehow, top of the league. 41 points, the week's worst return, with 11 more stranded on a bench that never got the call. Haaland delivered the standard 18, but nothing else fired. It's a comedown from a big Gameweek 2 (103 points, Haaland brace and all), but three weeks in, the lead built early is still just about holding. The armour is cracking. The crown, for now, stays on.

The Headlines

  • Jianguo FC's wildcard rebuild delivers the week's best score — 70, and a climb to 2nd.
  • Pending Moderation posts the week's worst score, 41, and still leads the league. The summit is shakier than it looks.
  • 14 points, gone, wasted, unclaimed — Daniel Dring's third straight gameweek leading the league in points benched.
  • Worst captain of the week: Tony Yeboah's Eye, with Bruno Fernandes managing just 2 points while Haaland ran riot elsewhere.
  • Not a single hit taken across the entire league, three gameweeks running. Nerve, or simply indecision.

Three gameweeks down. The summit is shakier than it looks.
