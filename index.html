The Match Report

1st

Michael Ball — Sao Baulo

There is a new name at the top of the table. Haaland scored twice and doubled to 26, Gakpo made way for Groß (5 points), and the bench cost just 2 — Janelt actually scored −1 while sitting on it. 78 was the best score of the week, a fourth gameweek win of the season, and for the first time all year Simon Edwards is not first. Sao Baulo leads by five. The chase is over. The defence begins.

2nd

Jonathan Kane — Real Moat

Off the bottom at last. 70 points, the second-best score of the week, with Haaland's 26 doing the heavy lifting and no transfers needed after last week's Wildcard. Eighteen points still sat on the bench — Calvert-Lewin 9, Suzuki 8 — but the table has finally noticed: Real Moat climbs to 5th on 390, leaving the foot of the table where it had sat since Gameweek 2.

3rd

Joel Xavier — Jianguo FC

A sensible armband, at last. After last week's Bruno Fernandes misadventure, Haaland wears it and returns 26. Willock out, Groß in, a gain of 4 — the same move Sao Baulo made. 63 points, 15 left on the bench with Castagne's 8 the biggest chunk. Holds 3rd on 420, with the most valuable squad in the league at £101.2m.

4th

Simon Edwards — Pending Moderation

Six gameweeks at the top. Now over. It wasn't a bad week: 61 points, Haaland's 26, a tidy 5 on the bench, and no transfers again. But a good score isn't enough when the person behind you posts 78. Pending Moderation drops to 2nd on 441, five points adrift. The hermit has been found, and he is no longer in the lead.

5th

Daniel Dring — The Two Time

Evanilson strikes again — from the bench. 60 points, Haaland's 26, no transfers (none since Gameweek 2), and 14 points left unused, 12 of them Evanilson's. That takes his tally to 37 points scored for The Two Time without once being picked, across seven gameweeks. Slips to 6th — last place for the first time this season, six points behind Real Moat.

6th

Michael Desmond — Tony Yeboah's Eye

Best transfer and worst captain, same man, same week. Delap out, Calvert-Lewin in, and the swap returned 9 points: a gain of 7. Then Palmer wore the armband, managed 2, doubled to 4, while vice-captain Ødegaard sat on 3. 53 points, the lowest of the week, with just 3 on the bench. Calvert-Lewin also scored 9 on Jonathan Kane's bench, which helps nobody. Holds 4th on 394.
