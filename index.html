The Match Report

1st

Michael Ball — Sao Baulo

No fuss, no theatre — just the week's best score. 74 points, a single tidy transfer (Thiago out, João Pedro in, who repaid the faith with 12), Haaland doing the standard 18 as captain, and just 1 point wasted on the bench. Quietly, this is now three gameweeks of exactly the kind of squad management that wins leagues. Climbs to 2nd overall, +2.

2nd

Simon Edwards — Pending Moderation

The bounce-back, right on schedule. After the worst score of anyone in Gameweek 3, 72 points restores order — Haaland's usual 18, a tidy 3 on the bench, and, notably, still not a single transfer made all season. Four gameweeks in and the squad remains untouched. Stays top of the league, extends the total to 273.

3rd

Michael Desmond — Tony Yeboah's Eye

Finally, a captain that isn't Bruno Fernandes — and a much steadier week for it. Palmer wore the armband and returned 10, a far cry from the 46-point high of Gameweek 2 or the 4-point crash that followed it, but reliable this time. Less reliable: 14 points left on the bench, with Van Hecke's 8 the biggest waste. Holds at 3rd overall.

4th

Jonathan Kane — Real Moat

The only hit of the gameweek, and it paid off. A four-point deduction for two transfers, Rogers brought in and handed the armband straight away — he returned 8, doubled to 16, the standout pick of the week. 64 points net, a fourth straight respectable score. And yet — Real Moat sits 6th again. Four gameweeks, four decent totals, still stuck at the bottom. The table still hasn't noticed.

5th

Daniel Dring — The Two Time

A rare quiet week for the league's bench villain. Just 7 points left unused this time — by far the tidiest bench Daniel Dring has managed all season, after 15, 19, and 14 in the three gameweeks prior. 57 points, no transfers, and for once the team sheet isn't the story. Holds at 5th overall.

6th

Joel Xavier — Jianguo FC

The wildcard hangover arrives. A week after rebuilding the entire squad and topping the gameweek table, Jianguo FC posts the league's worst score — 55 — with the league's worst captain pick (Isak, just 2 points, doubled to 4) and the league's worst bench waste, 17 points, Leno alone accounting for 9 of it from a benched goalkeeper. Slips to 4th overall, -2. The rebuild bought a great week. This wasn't it.

The Headlines

  • Sao Baulo posts the week's best score, 74, with a single transfer and almost nothing wasted on the bench.
  • Simon Edwards still hasn't made a single transfer all season — four gameweeks in, and still top of the league.
  • The wildcard hangover: Jianguo FC follows last week's high with the league's worst score, worst captain, and worst bench of the gameweek.
  • Real Moat's second hit of the season pays off — 64 net — and it still isn't enough to climb off the bottom.
  • Michael Desmond finally captains someone other than Bruno Fernandes. The result: no fireworks, no crash, just 10 solid points.

Four gameweeks down. Simon Edwards's lead is intact — for now.
