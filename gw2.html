The Match Report

1st

Simon Edwards — Pending Moderation

103 points, and the league has never seen a start like it. Haaland scored twice, doubled to 26 for the armband, and the rest of the squad simply refused to let up. No transfers, no fuss — just a bench of 13 as the only blemish. The quirk of the week: on paper, Haaland's return was the joint-lowest of any captain picked in the league. Didn't matter one bit. Stays top, extends the lead.

2nd

Michael Desmond — Tony Yeboah's Eye

A four-point hit, two transfers, and the gamble of the season so far. Palmer and Rogers brought in, Cunha and Gibbs-White shown the door — then Bruno Fernandes wore the armband and delivered a hat-trick and an assist, 23 points doubled to a scarcely believable 46. 97 net, the week's second-best score, a bench of just 1 point wasted. And yet — the league moves fast. Slips to 3rd overall regardless.

3rd

Joel Xavier — Jianguo FC

The same Bruno Fernandes explosion, the same 46-point captain haul — just with less fanfare. One clean transfer, Gyökeres out for João Pedro, who repaid the faith with 9. 88 points, 8 more left unused on the bench. Climbs to 2nd overall — the first real sign that this squad means business.

4th

Daniel Dring — The Two Time

75 points on the pitch, but the real story is what got left off it. Dedić was brought in specifically this week — and immediately parked on the bench, where he banked 11 points doing absolutely nothing for the score. 19 points shed in total, the worst bench return anywhere in the league. Slips to 5th overall. The transfer worked. The team sheet didn't.

5th

Jonathan Kane — Real Moat

A genuinely solid week, and it still isn't enough. 69 points, Haaland doing his usual duty, no transfers to complicate matters — and Real Moat remains stuck at the foot of the table regardless, a second respectable score in a row that the standings simply haven't rewarded yet.

6th

Michael Ball — Sao Baulo

The week's lowest score — and somehow a climb up the table anyway. 65 points, one minor swap (Sarr for Janelt), and the tightest bench in the league at just 4 points wasted. Proof that tidy squad management can still move you up a place, even on your worst week. Climbs to 4th overall.

The Headlines

  • Two Bruno Fernandes armbands, two 46-point returns — Tony Yeboah's Eye and Jianguo FC both cash in on the same captaincy call.
  • 19 points, gone, wasted, unclaimed — Daniel Dring's bench outscores half the league's captains.
  • A 4-point hit that paid off in spades: Tony Yeboah's Eye's gamble nets the week's second-best score.
  • Sao Baulo posts the week's worst score, 65, and still climbs the table. The tightest bench in the league does the rest.

Two gameweeks down. The story's only getting started.
